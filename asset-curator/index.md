Managing physical equipment, tools, and valuable property often turns into guesswork when systems rely on memory or scattered spreadsheets. **The Asset Curator (TAC)** is built to solve that exact problem. It gives individuals, small businesses, and teams a practical, dependable system to know precisely what they own, where each item currently sits, what condition it’s in, and when it requires maintenance.

Whether you are organizing expensive tools across job sites, managing shared company equipment, or keeping a tight home inventory, TAC bridges the gap between your physical assets and digital records.

---

### **Built Around Real-World Workflows**

<p><strong>Physical-to-Digital Tracking with Integrated QR Codes</strong><br>
Every asset in TAC gets a unique, auto-generated ID and standard QR code. Print labels straight from the app to tag your physical gear. Scanning an item’s label instantly pulls up its full history on screen, making stocktakes, handovers, and site checks fast and error-free.</p>

<p><strong>Separate "Home" and "Current" Locations</strong><br>
Physical gear rarely stays in one place. TAC tracks where an item <em>normally belongs</em> alongside where it is <em>right now</em>. This simple distinction makes it effortless to spot gear that is currently out on loan, dispatched to a job site, or out for repair.</p>

<p><strong>Complete Lifecycle & Maintenance Management</strong><br>
Asset tracking shouldn't stop at data entry. TAC’s Pro tier allows you to log routine service schedules based on dates, distance, hours, or usage cycles. Keep equipment operational and catch overdue maintenance before costly breakdowns happen.</p>

<p><strong>Flexibility: Start Local, Scale to Shared Teams</strong><br>
Start immediately in Local Mode with zero setup or cloud accounts—your data stays 100% on your device. As your operations grow, unlock Master Database sharing to sync records seamlessly across multiple devices and team members.</p>

---

<h3 style="color: #0f172a;">Key Capabilities at a Glance</h3>

* **Instant Search & Filtering:** Filter items rapidly by custom tags, current location, or operational status.
* **Visual & Detailed Records:** Store photos, purchase values, serial numbers, part numbers, and update timestamps.
* **Reporting & Data Export:** Generate operational summaries and export database records to Excel whenever you need audits or backups.
* **Label Printing Built-In:** Generate ready-to-print labels directly from your filtered inventory views.
