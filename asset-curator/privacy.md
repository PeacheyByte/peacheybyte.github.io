### **Information Collection and Use**

To provide maintenance tracking, QR code management, and multi-user data sharing, **The Asset Curator** utilizes specific device permissions and cloud services:

* **Camera & Media Access:** The app requests access to your device camera and storage purely to scan QR codes and capture or attach asset photographs. 
* **User Accounts & Shared Data:** User credentials and shared asset database records are securely handled through **Google Firebase**. Personal information is never sold, leased, or rented to third parties.
