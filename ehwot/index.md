EhWot is a lightweight, easy-to-use text translation app designed for quick, direct translations without clutter or unnecessary complexity.

### Key Features

* **Simple & Fast:** Input your text and get clear translations immediately.
* **Lightweight:** Minimal storage footprint on your device.
* **No Clutter:** Designed purely for focus and quick readability.
